# Value Investing Templates

Use these templates for memos, reviews, and checklists.

## 1. 100-Point Scorecard

| Module | Points | What To Check |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| circle of competence | 10 | product, customer, competition, profit source |
| industry position | 15 | space, cycle, supply/demand, policy |
| company quality | 20 | moat, management, leader status, model |
| financial quality | 15 | ROE, cash flow, debt, profit quality, dividends/buybacks |
| growth logic | 10 | 3-5 year profit path |
| valuation margin | 20 | payback period, percentile, downside case |
| market season | 5 | sentiment, liquidity, macro water level |
| behavioral fit | 5 | can the investor hold through a 30% drawdown? |

Action guide:

| Score | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 85+ | core candidate; still build gradually |
| 75-84 | investable/watch closely; moderate size |
| 65-74 | wait for clearer logic or better price |
| <65 | do not buy |

One-vote veto:

- business not understood
- management integrity concern
- poor cash flow with heavy storytelling
- high leverage plus cycle downturn
- valuation depends on extreme growth
- reason is only "it is going up" or "everyone is buying"

## 2. Company Research Template

```text
Company:
Industry:
One-sentence business:

1. How does it make money?
2. Who are the customers and is demand durable?
3. How large is the industry space?
4. Who are the top competitors?
5. What is the moat?
6. What are the last 3 years of revenue, profit, ROE, and cash flow?
7. Where will growth come from?
8. What is the largest risk?
9. Is management trustworthy and rational?
10. What is the fair valuation range?
11. What expectations are implied by current price?
12. What would justify adding?
13. What would force selling?
```

## 7. Anti-Emotion Playbook

### During A Market Crash

Ask:

1. Is the crash from a high valuation, fair valuation, or low valuation zone?
2. Did the business fundamentals worsen?
3. Did the industry permanently deteriorate?
4. Is there cash to continue buying?
5. Is this risk release or the start of risk?

Actions:

| Context | Action |
| --- | --- |
| high-valuation crash | protect capital |
| mid-cycle crash | observe or buy small |
| low-valuation crash | add according to plan |

### After Missing A Bull Move

Avoid:

- all-in chasing from anxiety
- waiting in cash for a perfect crash

Prefer:

- look for less crowded undervaluation
- use broad indices for market exposure
- enter gradually
- accept missing leading sectors; do not miss the whole season

### When A Holding Underperforms

| Underperformance Type | Action |
| --- | --- |
| thesis intact, style out of favor | hold or adjust mildly |
| thesis weakened | reduce size and wait for evidence |
| thesis invalidated | sell; do not hide behind long-termism |

### After Making Money

Check:

- Was the return skill or market beta?
- Has valuation become stretched?
- Am I tempted to use leverage?
- Am I lowering research standards?
- Am I confusing luck with ability?

Many major losses begin after a period of easy wins.
